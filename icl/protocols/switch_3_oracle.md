# Protocol (3 prisoners, 6 days)

Meaning of the bulb: **ON means "at least two different prisoners have already been in the room".**

When you enter the room, apply the first rule that matches:

1. The bulb is ON and this is your first visit → **ANNOUNCE**.
   (At least two others have visited before you, and you are the third.)
2. The bulb is ON → leave it **ON**.
3. It is not day 1 and you were **not** the prisoner in the room yesterday → turn it **ON**.
   (Yesterday's prisoner was someone else, so together with you at least two different prisoners have visited.)
4. Otherwise → leave it **OFF**.

Never announce in any other situation.
